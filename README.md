### [Office Furniture Sales & Operations Analytics | Power BI, DAX, Python, SQL](https://express-js-on-vercel-three-delta-85.vercel.app/projects/office-furniture-ecommerce-analysis)

### [ITB Customer Financial Health Analysis | SQL, Python, Excel, scikit-learn](https://github.com/AIVIETNAM-AIO-thuhuynhtran05/ITB-Customer-Financial-Health-Analysis)
Finalist case at the ITB Competition (UEL): helped a consumer-finance company understand customers' financial wellbeing, spending, and channel engagement from 1.85M card transactions of 999 customers (synthetic 2025 data). Found that spend-to-income alone explains 81% of the health score, segmented customers with K-Means (ARI 0.97 across 50 bootstrap samples) into 5 groups, and designed 6 non-punitive interventions in a 10-week rollout covering 90.9% of customers.

### [VinDatathon 2026 – E-commerce Business Analytics | Power BI, DAX, Python, Excel](https://github.com/AIVIETNAM-AIO-thuhuynhtran05/Vin-Datathon)
Top 20 out of 500 teams. Built a 5-page Power BI dashboard on 10 years of simulated Vietnamese fashion e-commerce data (2012–2022, 15 CSV files, ~647K orders) and showed the decline came from conversion (0.13%) and retention, not traffic. Turned the findings into 5 prioritized action areas with tracking KPIs.

### [Airbnb Los Angeles Data Warehouse & OLAP Analysis | SQL Server, SSIS, SSAS, Power BI, Python](https://github.com/AIVIETNAM-AIO-thuhuynhtran05/AirBnB-of-Los-Angeles-Data-Warehouse-OLAP-Multidimensional-Analysis-)
Turned raw Airbnb Los Angeles CSV files (11,996 listings) into a SQL Server data warehouse that can be queried directly with SQL for analysis and reporting: a star schema loaded through SSIS, an SSAS cube with four dimensions, and a Power BI report. Trained three models to classify Superhosts; Random Forest reached 90.19% ROC-AUC.

### [Personalized Federated Graph Learning for Credit Risk Prediction | Python, PyTorch Geometric, LightGBM](https://github.com/AIVIETNAM-AIO-thuhuynhtran05/Personalized-Federated-Heterogeneous-Graph-Learning-for-Credit-Risk-Prediction-)
Graduation thesis (in progress since August 2026). Integrated 7 Home Credit tables (307,511 customers, 26.3M records) into a customer credit graph and 601 tabular features. Simulated 5 lenders without sharing raw data: the federated model reached 0.7836 ROC-AUC, on par with centralized training (0.7858) and better than local-only models at 4 of 5 lenders.
